Welcome to the Garden

The Riverside Community Garden has been growing since 2009. We think it is the best place in town to get your hands dirty.
Membership is open to everyone. No experience required.

What You Can Grow

  • Tomatoes
  • Peppers
  • Sunflowers
  • Pumpkins
  • Herbs of all kinds

Rules of the Garden

  1. Water your plot at least twice a week.
  2. Compost goes in the green bin.
  3. Lock the gate when you leave.
