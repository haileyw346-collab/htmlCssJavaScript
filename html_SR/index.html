About the Game

Slime Rancher is esentially a farming game that controls like an fps. You have a vac-pack that lets you vaccum up objects like slimes and chickens, and then shoot them back out. On your ranch, there are plots that you can make into various things, such as a corral to keep slimes in one place or a garden to grow fruits and veggies.

Slimes

What are slimes?

Slimes are creatures found throughout the world of slime rancher that come in many types. Most types of slimes eat food from one of three general food types - fruit, veggie, and meat - with Pinks and Ringtails eating all three. There are also three special food types- water, ash, and nectar - each eaten by only one slime. Slimes are usually docile, but you can be hurt by some of them if you're not careful.

plorts

When a slime is fed, it produces a plort. Plorts can be sold, put into the refinery, or used to make largos. Slimes produce double the plorts when fed their favorite food.

Largos

A largo is created when a slime eats a plort it does not produce. Their diet is a combination of the parent types, keeping both types favorite food as well. When fed, largos produce one plort of each of its types.
In some cases largos can become feral and will try to eat you. Feral largos can be calmed by feeding them.
When a largo eats a plort it does not produce, it becomes a tarr, eating anything that moves including slimes, chickens, and you. they can be dispatched by either throwing them into the sea or by any kind of water.
Largos and tarr are too big to fit in the vac-pack, sticking to the nozzle instead. this prevents you from vacing up or shooting out other objects until whats on the nozzle is either dropped or shot off.
